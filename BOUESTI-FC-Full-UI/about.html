About the Club

A modern student football platform built around development, teamwork and the BOUESTI community.

HomeAbout The Club
Who We Are

Football with purpose

BOUESTI Football Club is presented here as the university football platform for students who want to compete, improve and represent the institution with discipline. This static template is designed so official club history, staff and verified records can be connected later through the backend.

The visual identity combines BOUESTI green and gold with a dark match-day atmosphere, creating a football experience that feels modern while remaining rooted in the university.

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

Provide an organised football environment that supports competition, learning, teamwork and student development.

Vision

Build a respected university football culture where talent and academic growth can exist side by side.

Character

Represent BOUESTI through discipline, respect, community and responsibility on and off the pitch.
